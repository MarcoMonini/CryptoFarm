Indicator zones

Where an indicator, inside a given band, says something about the price over the next N candles: every column of features, at five windows, on BTC and ETH, 5m and 15m. The zones are chosen on 2017–2020 and read, untouched, on 2021–2025 and on the months after.

Outcome

Of 115 zones chosen, 43 hold, and they are four phenomena

Many of the 43 zones are the same event seen through different indicators: a crash takes the distance from VWAP, the distance from the EMA, the EMA's slope and the return to the bottom together. The distinct phenomena are four, and only the first clears the fees consistently.

1 above the fee

Price far below its own average

The bottom 1% of the distance from VWAP, EMA, KAMA, PSAR or the recent high. It bounces on every column, on both timeframes, in all three periods. On 15m about +0.5% gross a trade in an hour and a half.

2 noisy

Huge bars

The top 1% of the range, of the wicks, of the volatility. The day after, the price rises, but on few trades and with errors as large as the means: these are the volatile periods of the rallies and the bounces after the crashes.

3 1/20 of the fee

Close at the low, one candle later

Statistically rock solid, over tens of thousands of trades, but worth +0.004% to +0.013%. It is the microstructure the spread lives on, and about a quarter of what it was in 2017–2020.

4 under the fee

Slow, small bounce

The close at the bottom 1% of the 48-candle window on 15m: +0.14% and +0.16% a trade, the most precise of all, and under 0.20%.

Method

Choose on some years, read on others

Looked at over the whole history, every heatmap of the decomposer has a coloured corner, and with tens of thousands of cells some are coloured by chance. That is why the choice and the reading sit on different periods, and why the criteria are written before looking at the second.

Discovery2017-08 → 2020-12 · zones and edges are chosen here
Confirm2021-01 → 2025-06 · read once, with everything frozen
Recent2025-06 → today
Indicators
The 29 columns of features. The 7 that read no window (return, body, range, wicks, close in the bar, PSAR) only once.
Parameters
The window N of features: 6, 12, 24, 48, 96 candles. It is the window of every average, RSI, ATR, VWAP of the column; the short window is a quarter of it.
Bands
Quantiles of the indicator measured on discovery: 1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 90%, 95%, 99%. The same edges on the other periods. The 1% tails are there to find narrow zones like "RSI 80–90", which deciles dilute.
Horizons
N = 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, 96, 192 candles after the close.
Measure
Cumulative return from the close to the close N candles later, minus that of every candle of the same period at the same N. Standard error over non-overlapping blocks of N candles.
Criterion
A zone passes discovery beyond 4 standard errors on BTC and on ETH with the same sign (roughly Bonferroni over 17,700 cells per asset). Of neighbouring windows and N the best cell is kept. It holds if on confirm it has the same sign on both beyond 2 standard errors.
Trades
One trade per episode: entry at the close of the first candle in the band, exit N candles later, no new entry before. Gross, against the 0.20% OKX round-trip fee.
What holds

Almost everything shrinks, a part stays above the threshold

Each point is one of the 115 zones. Across, how strong it was on discovery; up, how strong it is on confirm, in the same direction; for both measures the weaker asset counts. If nothing changed the points would sit on the diagonal: they all sit below it, and those above the 2-standard-error line hold.

None of the 115 zones is stronger on confirm than on discovery: that is how much of the discovery was chance, plus how much the market has changed. The zones at N = 1 were the strongest in 2017–2020 and fall the most: 8 of 45 hold, and they keep a median 5% of their standard errors; the others 35 of 70, with 42%.

What does not hold

A high RSI is no longer momentum

In the decomposer, over the whole history, RSI 80–90 on BTC 15m was followed by +0.56% in half a day. With the zones chosen on 2017–2020 the phenomenon was strong then, up to 6.4 standard errors, and fades after: on confirm it stays positive but under 2 on at least one of the two assets, and in the recent period it changes sign on ETH.

Caveats

What these numbers do not say

  • BTC and ETH are not two independent confirmations: their crashes fall on the same days.
  • The trade buys at the close of a crash candle, that is when spread and slippage are worst. 0.10% a side is the minimum, not the expected cost.
  • The 2021–2025 confirm period was read once, with criteria written beforehand. From here on it is no longer a period to choose on.
  • On the recent period family 1 fires rarely because the thresholds are in 2017–2020 units; its numbers neither confirm nor refute.
Next steps

From the zone to the rule

  • Bands in units of the period's volatility instead of fixed 2017–2020 quantiles, so family 1 fires again today.
  • Write the rule before pricing it: buy the dislocated close, hold N, with N and window chosen here on 15m.
  • Price it on the recent period and then in paper trading, with TradingVision's per-bet test in sequential.
