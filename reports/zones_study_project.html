Method

The same search, and one thing it cannot undo

Discovery2017-08 → 2020-12 · zones and edges are chosen here
Confirm2021-01 → 2025-06 · already read once, for the first study
Recent2025-06 → 2026-08
Indicators
The 13 columns of trading/project_features.py, at the project's secondary parameters (Bollinger 2σ, Keltner 2 ATR, smoothing 3, Ichimoku 9/26/52 scaled to the window, fast EMA a quarter of the slow). Two written columns were exact duplicates of the first study's and were dropped before the run: the stochastic's %K (the close's place in the window) and the Keltner position (the distance from the EMA in ATR, rescaled).
Left out
What the first study already covered (RSI, TSI, ADX, ATR, PSAR, volume against its median, the bar's range); the futures positioning, which has no discovery period; the cross-sectional rank, which is not a property of one pair.
Rules
Unchanged: windows 6–96, bands at the discovery quantiles 1/5/10/25/50/75/90/95/99%, N 1–192, beyond 4 standard errors on BTC and ETH with one sign to pass, the best window and N per band kept, 2 on confirm to hold, one trade an episode against a 0.20% round trip. The indicator is read at the close of the signal candle and the trade opens at the next candle's open, the first price anyone could act on; the first study entered at the signal's own close. On confirm and recent the two differ by 0.006% a trade at most; on discovery by up to 0.3%, from Binance's thin first months, when the next candle's first print could sit far from the close (48.5% on ETH on 2017-08-22). The choice of zones reads close to close and is not touched. A whole candle late costs 0.1 to 0.2% a trade on family 1 at 15m.
The catch
Confirm was read for the first study, and its answer is known. A new column that measures the same thing as a zone that held will hold too. That is why the table carries each zone's closest first-study column and its Spearman: a zone at ρ 0.98 is the old zone, not a second confirmation.

Caveats

What these numbers do not say

  • Confirm is no longer clean: it had been read once, and the columns here correlate with the ones that held there.
  • BTC and ETH are not two independent confirmations: their crashes fall on the same days.
  • The trades buy the close of a dislocated candle, where spread and slippage are worst. 0.10% a side is the minimum.
  • This store ends on 2026-08-19, seven weeks before TradingVision's: the recent period is shorter here.
Next steps

From the zone to the rule

  • Family 1 is now the same finding in seven units. Pick one, write the rule — buy the dislocated close on 15m, hold N — and price it on the recent period only.
  • Family 5 as a filter on family 1: does a bounce inside an up-run pay more than one inside a down-run?
  • Bands in units of the period's volatility, so the 2017–2020 tails fire again after 2025.
