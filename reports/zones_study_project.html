Indicators
The 13 columns of trading/project_features.py, at the project's secondary parameters (Bollinger 2σ, Keltner 2 ATR, smoothing 3, Ichimoku 9/26/52 scaled to the window, fast EMA a quarter of the slow). Two written columns were exact duplicates of the first study's and were dropped before the run: the stochastic's %K (the close's place in the window) and the Keltner position (the distance from the EMA in ATR, rescaled).
Left out
What the first study already covered (RSI, TSI, ADX, ATR, PSAR, volume against its median, the bar's range); the futures positioning, which has no discovery period; the cross-sectional rank, which is not a property of one pair.
Rules
Unchanged: windows 6–96, bands at the discovery quantiles 1/5/10/25/50/75/90/95/99%, N 1–192, beyond 4 standard errors on BTC and ETH with one sign to pass, the best window and N per band kept, 2 on confirm to hold, one trade an episode against a 0.20% round trip. The indicator is read at the close of the signal candle; one candle goes by, to see it and act, and the trade opens at the next open and is held N candles. Beside it, entering at the next open with no candle of delay. The first study entered at the signal's own close: against the next open that differs by 0.006% a trade at most on confirm and recent, and by up to 0.3% on discovery, from Binance's thin first months, when the next candle's first print could sit far from the close (48.5% on ETH on 2017-08-22). The choice of zones reads close to close and is not touched by either.
The catch
Confirm was read for the first study, and its answer is known. A new column that measures the same thing as a zone that held will hold too. That is why the table carries each zone's closest first-study column and its Spearman: a zone at ρ 0.98 is the old zone, not a second confirmation.

Caveats

What these numbers do not say

  • Confirm is no longer clean: it had been read once, and the columns here correlate with the ones that held there.
  • BTC and ETH are not two independent confirmations: their crashes fall on the same days.
  • The trades buy a candle after a dislocated one, when spread and slippage are still wide. 0.10% a side is the minimum.
  • Which candles of a crash are taken moves family 1 a lot: letting the next trade start on the previous one's exit candle instead of after it takes the 15m EMA spread from +0.36 to +0.50% on BTC, on 21 signals. The rule here is the first study's.
  • This store ends on 2026-08-19, seven weeks before TradingVision's: the recent period is shorter here.
Next steps

From the zone to the rule

  • Family 1 is the same finding in seven units, and its edge lives in the first candle. A rule worth writing has to act inside it — a limit order resting under the price, filled during the dislocated candle — and be priced on the recent period only.
  • Family 5 as a filter on family 1: does a bounce inside an up-run pay more than one inside a down-run?
  • Bands in units of the period's volatility, so the 2017–2020 tails fire again after 2025.
